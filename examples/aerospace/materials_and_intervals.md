# Materials and Maintenance Intervals

This fictional TF-40 fleet record demonstrates how maintenance knowledge connects specifications, parts, and inspection planning. It is sample data for software testing. Material Traceability requires Procurement Record. Titanium Fan Blade requires Ti-6Al-4V. The example drawing calls for a titanium product per AMS-4928, commonly written as AMS 4928 in purchasing records. Always verify the actual specification revision and product form against the approved drawing.

## Traceability and specification control

A material designation is not a complete part definition. Product form, heat treatment, processing history, dimensions, and inspection requirements affect whether supplied stock satisfies an order. Procurement Record requires Material Certificate. Material Certificate requires Heat Number. Record the supplier, certificate identifier, heat or lot number, and the drawing revision used for acceptance. Preserve the connection between incoming material and each serialized finished component.

Ti-6Al-4V offers a useful strength-to-weight balance for appropriate compressor and fan applications, but the alloy name does not authorize a substitution. A part designed for a different environment may use another alloy or coating system. Material Substitution requires Engineering Approval. Engineering Approval requires Drawing Review. Repairs also need their own approved instructions; a base material certificate does not establish that a repaired geometry meets its requirements.

## Planning intervals

Maintenance Interval requires Cycle Tracking. Cycle Tracking requires Maintenance Records. This demonstration uses a 500-cycle planning review for ordinary fan leading edge observations and a 250-cycle review for engines operating in an unusually dusty training scenario. These numbers are invented scheduling examples. Actual inspection intervals and mandatory retirement limits must be obtained from applicable approved instructions and operational requirements.

Keep flight hours, starts, flight cycles, and exceptional events distinct. A component's controlled life counter may follow a definition that differs from the fleet dashboard's generic cycle field. Reconcile records after component transfer and preserve the source of each adjustment. A missing history should trigger the approved records resolution process rather than an estimated serviceable life.

## Integrating findings

Inspection Planning requires Exposure Review. Sand Ingestion causes Fan Blade Erosion. Thermal Fatigue causes Surface Cracking. Combining these links helps an investigator retrieve relevant work cards and historical observations. It does not automatically shorten or extend a mandated interval. Document the reason for any authorized additional inspection and retain the resulting findings. Use consistent names for parts and defects in records so search results can connect inspection evidence, procurement certificates, and the engineering disposition without losing the original source context.
